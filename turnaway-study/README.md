Mentor: Jonathan Larson

Reflecting on the Turnaway Study: A Literature Review

Conducted a statistical evaluation of the Turnaway Study, which utilized linear and logistic mixed effects models to analyze the mental, physical, and socioeconomic outcomes of abortion access. Focused on study design, handling unbalanced longitudinal data, and accounting for within-facility and individual-level correlations. Explored the implications of abortion restrictions post-Roe v. Wade and provided a statistical foundation for future research on reproductive health policy.
