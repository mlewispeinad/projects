Research Question

  • How do squirrel-human interactions differ across various NYC parks?
    • Interested in environmental and atmosphere/ambiance factors
    • Squirrels are important part of the NYC ecosystem & health of urban green spaces
  • Our hypothesis: Busier parks will have braver squirrels!
    • Adaptation from exposure

Our Dataset

  • Dataset source: The Squirrel Census sample from March 1st, 2020
  • 3 Datasets: Park dataset, Squirrel dataset, & Park Info dataset (NYCgovparks.org, made by us)
    • Left-joined by common variables (area name, area ID, park name, and park ID)
  • Cleaned data mainly using grepl() function
    • for some variables, people were allowed to write additional comments

Our Dataset (cont.)

  • Park Dataset:
    • Park conditions (boolean: calm or busy)
    • Area name: area where the park is located (upper manhattan, central manhattan, lower manhattan, or brooklyn)
    • Area ID: A, B, C, or D assigned to an area name
    • Park name: name of the park
    • Park ID: 1-24 assigned to park names
    • Number of squirrels: squirrel count at specific park
    • Litter (some, none, abundant)

Our Dataset (cont.)

  • Squirrel Dataset:
    • Area name, Area ID, Park name, Park ID
    • Primary fur color: gray, black, or cinnamon
    • Squirrel longitude: longitude coordinate for observed squirrel
    • Squirrel latitude: latitude coordinate for observed squirrel
    • Interactions with Humans: approaches, indifferent, or runs from
    • Activities: value is either “running”, “chasing”, “climbing”, or “foraging”
    • Location: location when first sighted (value is either “ground plane”, “above ground”, or specific location)

Our Dataset (cont.)

  • Park Info Dataset:
    • Park name
    • Park ID
    • Total acres
    • Acres of natural areas
    • Number of mapped trees
  • Helped us group parks
  • We manually created the following variables:
    • Squirrel Rate: Number of Squirrels / Total Acres
    • Rate Group: Quantiles of Squirrel Rate
    • Park Size: Quantiles of Total Acres
